How we work

Three steps, one contact

No time on the phone with suppliers and no paperwork for your team. You close the deal; we get the utilities ready.

  1. You send us the client

    When you close a sale or a rental, you send us the buyer's or tenant's details.

  2. We handle the utilities

    New connection, change of account holder or change of supplier for electricity and gas, on the tariff that fits best.

  3. You earn your commission

    Your agency earns a commission for every new contract we handle. We let you know when everything is done.
